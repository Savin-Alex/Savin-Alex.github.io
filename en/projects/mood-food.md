**2025–2026 · Yandex Practicum, IT Project Manager program · Capstone project, team of 4 · Project defended**

Mood&Food is a mobile app for waiters in a restaurant chain. A team of four took the project through the full cycle, from the brief to the solution defense.

## The challenge

A restaurant chain needs an app for its waiters, but off-the-shelf systems cost about ₽260,000–1,600,000 a year for a 13-location chain and often include features it does not need. Without prioritization, the product turns into a long wish list. The task was to reduce a broad idea to a manageable first release with a clear scope, estimate and schedule.

## My contribution

**IT Project Manager / Business Analyst in a team of 4: responsible for requirements, prioritization, the estimate and the project plan.**

- Clarified the product idea and project constraints and analyzed 15 competing systems (Saby Presto, iiko, r_keeper and others).
- Gathered requirements and the feature list, prioritized the features with MoSCoW and defined the MVP scope.
- Prepared the project charter, commercial proposal, estimate, GanttPRO schedule, risk register and Jira backlog.
- Built an interactive Figma prototype and defended the solution before reviewers.

## What I delivered

- **MVP scope:** sign-in, home screen, menu, ordering, a loyalty program and other first-release features.
- **Offline mode:** the MVP architecture includes a local database, data sync and an operation queue.
- **Design and platform:** the MVP scope covers the design of all screens and development in React Native.
- **Later releases:** biometrics, menu search, table booking, an employee account and POS integrations were estimated separately.

## The result

**The team prepared a first-release plan — MVP scope, estimate, schedule, risk assessment and prototype — and defended it before reviewers.**

- The MVP scope was selected from 40+ features with MoSCoW, and a roadmap for later releases was prepared.
- The MVP estimate is about ₽5.27M, with a work schedule to match.
- A register of 50+ risks with probability, severity, strategy and response plan.

## Next steps

This was a training project: development and release were out of scope. Development would first need detailed analysis and a refined estimate, because the current one is based on high-level requirements.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Details: decisions and risks</summary>

- **Underestimated backend.** Plan: re-estimate from detailed requirements and allocate extra resources.
- **Fixed price without a buffer.** The budget can be overrun by 30–70%. Plan: a detailed specification and accurate estimate first, a mixed pricing model and a buffer in the plan.
- **"Everything at once" without prioritization.** The interface becomes complex and hard to use. Plan: a MoSCoW workshop, strict MVP boundaries and a product owner with veto rights.
